## Hi there! 👋


Welcome to my blog — a place for IT-Security that prefers context over headlines.

This space is dedicated to the careful analysis of adversary behavior, malicious infrastructure, and the often inconvenient details that turn raw observations into usable intelligence. Rather than treating indicators as facts in isolation, the focus lies on interpretation: how signals relate, where assumptions begin, and why some conclusions deserve a second look.

Much of the work presented here revolves around malware analysis and infrastructure research, with a particular interest in how threats communicate, evolve, and reuse patterns over time. Attribution, clustering, and framework design appear not as answers, but as questions — occasionally useful ones — that benefit from skepticism and iteration.

Threat hunting and detection emerge naturally from this process, but only as a consequence of understanding, not as a checkbox exercise. The intention is simple: fewer absolutes, more reasoning, and an appreciation for the uncomfortable ambiguity that real-world threat intelligence tends to produce.
